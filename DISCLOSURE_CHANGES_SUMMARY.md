## 🎯 **New Messaging Strategy**

### **From Legal/Defensive → To Value/Trust**

**Old Approach:**
- ❌ "Affiliate Disclosure:"
- ❌ "This site contains affiliate links"
- ❌ Legal/compliance focused

**New Approach:**
- ✅ "Trusted Reviews"
- ✅ "Pet Parent Approved"
- ✅ "Planet-Friendly Picks"
- ✅ Trust/value focused

- **Reinforces Mission**: "Planet-Friendly Picks" aligns with your brand

### **Footer Benefits:**
- **Highlights Quality**: "Expert reviews"
- **Shows Process**: "Thoroughly tested"
- **Reinforces Values**: "Sustainably sourced"

### **Disclosure Page Benefits:**
- **Positive Framing**: "Our Promise" vs "Affiliate Disclosure"
