## 🚀 **How to Use:**

### **Quick Start:**
1. Go to your admin panel: `http://127.0.0.1:8088/admin`
2. Scroll to "🤖 AI Article Generator" section
3. Select your options or click "💡 Get Content Ideas" for inspiration
4. Click "🤖 Generate AI Article"

1. **Product Category**: "Dog Toys"
2. **Article Type**: "Comparison Guide" 
3. **Content Angle**: "Budget Friendly"
4. **Target Audience**: "First-Time Pet Owners"
5. **SEO Strategy**: "Long-tail Keywords"
6. **Click "Get Content Ideas"** → Get 5 custom ideas
7. **Click an idea** → Auto-fills topic
8. **Generate Article** → AI creates targeted content
