## General UGC Tips for AI Creators:

### Tone & Style:
- Conversational and authentic
- Use "I" statements and personal experiences
- Include specific details and numbers
- Show genuine enthusiasm
- Address pain points directly

### Visual Suggestions:
- Show products in use
- Before/after comparisons
- Close-ups of quality/durability
- Your "dog" interacting with products
- Side-by-side product comparisons

### Engagement Hooks:
- Start with a question or surprising statement
- Use "Real talk" or "Can we talk about..."
- Reference common problems dog parents face
- Include specific timeframes and results

### Call-to-Action Variations:
- "Link in bio"
- "I've dropped the link below"
- "Link's in the comments"
- "Swipe up for details"
- "Check my stories for the direct link"
