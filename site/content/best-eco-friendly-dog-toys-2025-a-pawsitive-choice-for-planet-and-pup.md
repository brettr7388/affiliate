## What Makes a Dog Toy Eco-Friendly?

When searching for eco-friendly dog toys, consider these key factors:

* **Sustainable Materials:** Look for toys made from recycled materials, organic cotton, natural rubber, sustainably harvested wood, or other renewable resources. Avoid toys made from PVC, phthalates, and other harmful chemicals.
* **Biodegradability:** Opt for toys that are biodegradable or compostable at the end of their life cycle. This minimizes the environmental impact once your pup has outgrown their favorite chew toy.
* **Ethical Production:**  Support companies committed to ethical labor practices and fair trade principles.  Look for certifications that verify sustainable sourcing and manufacturing.
* **Durability:** While eco-friendly doesn't always mean flimsy, choosing durable toys extends their lifespan, reducing the need for frequent replacements.
