Mastering the Leash: Tips for First-Time Owners

Using a leash effectively is essential for your dog's safety and training. Here are a few tips for first-time pet owners:

  • Start with short walks: Gradually increase the duration of your walks as your dog gets used to the leash.
  • Use positive reinforcement: Reward your dog with treats and praise for good behavior.
  • Maintain a loose leash: Avoid pulling on the leash, as this can be uncomfortable for your dog and can lead to behavioral problems.
  • Supervise your dog at all times: Never leave your dog unattended while they are on a leash.
